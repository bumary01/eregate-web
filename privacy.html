Eregate Innovations Limited ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website (eregateinnovations.com) and use our services.

  • Contact Forms: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and message content

  • Account Information: If you create an account, login credentials and profile information

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: IP address, browser type, operating system, device type

2.3 Third-Party Information

We may receive information about you from third-party sources such as business partners, marketers, and social media platforms (if you interact with us through these channels).

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, maintain, and improve our Services
  • To respond to your inquiries and customer service requests
  • To process transactions and send related information
  • To send promotional communications (with your consent)
  • To analyze website usage and performance
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent activity and security issues
  • To comply with legal obligations and regulations

4.1 We Do Not Sell Your Data

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

4.2 Information We May Share

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy legal, accounting, or reporting requirements. Retention periods vary based on context and purpose:

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time, subject to legal obligations.
